Delete Your Account

The canonical copy of this page lives at prabhasaaho213-rgb.github.io/macro-snap/delete-account.html. You are being redirected there automatically; the instructions below are repeated here so this address keeps working on its own.

What gets deleted:
  • Your phone number and authentication data
  • Your meal history and nutrition logs
  • Your streak data and preferences
  • Your subscription status (if any)
What is retained (if applicable):
  • Anonymous crash reports (no personal identifiers)
  • Transaction records for tax/accounting purposes (90 days)

Option 1: Delete Inside the App

Open Settings in MacroSnap and tap Delete Account. This is the fastest route: your account and all associated data are removed immediately, and you are signed out.

Option 2: Request via Email

Send an email from the address associated with your account:

To: macrosnap7@gmail.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request
Body: Please include your registered phone number and the reason for deletion (optional).

Send Deletion Request Email

Processing Time

In-app requests are processed immediately. Email requests are processed within 7 business days, and you receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.
